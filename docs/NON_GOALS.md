# Product non-goals

- Executing tools, authorizing business actions, or proving semantic correctness.
- LLM-generated repairs, missing-value invention, fuzzy keys, or arbitrary coercion.
- A second independent Python enforcement engine.
- Claiming that the local managed control plane is production hosting, payment settlement, public ingress, external delivery, or an SLA.
- Collecting raw cross-customer argument payloads or releasing intelligence below the configured tenant privacy threshold.
- Supporting every framework version or JSON Schema extension.
- Claims of product-market fit, willingness to pay, or universal competitor absence.

The initial field test asks whether real teams encounter repair or drift cases that basic validation cannot resolve. The intended product is broader: offline enforcement plus managed conformance intelligence, audit trust, and daily operational workflow. Those ambitions do not convert unimplemented hosted capabilities or uncollected production evidence into current claims.
